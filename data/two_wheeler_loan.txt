Simple loans. Clear terms. Fast decisions.

Overview
A Two-Wheeler Loan helps you finance the purchase of a scooter or motorcycle through affordable monthly EMIs. It is typically secured against the vehicle and is designed to keep your upfront cost manageable while enabling ownership sooner.

Who this loan is for
• First-time buyers who want manageable EMIs
• Working professionals who need daily commuting support
• Small business owners who need mobility for operations
• Customers upgrading to a new model with better mileage/safety features

Key features
• Competitive financing for eligible profiles
• Quick decisions at partner dealerships (where available)
• Flexible down payment options (policy and model dependent)
• Tenors designed for practical monthly EMIs
• Transparent breakup of on-road price, down payment, loan amount, and EMI

What costs can be financed
Depending on the program and dealership structure, financing can cover:
• Ex-showroom price (primary component)
• Certain add-ons (extended warranty, accessories) if included in invoice and permitted
• Insurance and registration are usually paid separately, but bundled options may exist
Always confirm the exact invoice components included in your financed amount.

Eligibility (high-level)
Eligibility may consider:
• Age and residency criteria
• Income stability and monthly repayment capacity
• Credit profile and repayment track record
• Employer/business stability (as applicable)
• Vehicle model eligibility and on-road price

Documents commonly requested
KYC: identity and address proof.
Income proof: salary slips, bank statement, or business cashflow proof.
Photograph and signatures (as required).
Dealership documents: proforma invoice and vehicle details.

Loan structure and vehicle hypothecation
Two-wheeler loans are generally secured by the financed vehicle. The vehicle registration may show hypothecation in favor of the lender until the loan is fully repaid. Once you complete repayment, you can request hypothecation removal through the process specified by local transport authorities and your lender.

Interest rate/APR and fees
Pricing depends on:
• Credit profile
• Down payment level
• Tenor and loan amount
Fees can include processing fee, documentation charges, and penalties for missed payments or mandate failures. Exact details are shown in your KFS/loan schedule.

Repayment basics
• EMIs are typically paid monthly through auto-debit or partner payment channels.
• Keep sufficient balance to avoid bounce charges.
• In case of a missed EMI, pay at the earliest and contact support to avoid repeated penalties.

Insurance and safety
Insurance is important for legal compliance and financial protection. We encourage customers to maintain valid insurance cover for the loan tenor. Some programs may offer add-on protection; it is usually optional, unless mandated by policy or regulation.

Tips to choose the right EMI plan
• Keep EMI within a comfortable monthly budget.
• Consider total cost of ownership: fuel, maintenance, service, and insurance.
• Prefer shorter tenors if you can afford slightly higher EMIs; it reduces total interest paid.
