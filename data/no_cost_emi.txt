What is No-Cost EMI?
No-Cost EMI is a financing option offered at checkout with partner merchants where you pay the product price in equal monthly installments without bearing the usual interest burden—because the interest component is typically offset by a merchant-funded discount (often called a “subvention”). In simple words: you convert a full payment into EMIs while keeping the effective cost close to paying upfront, subject to plan terms.

How it works (simple explanation)
1) You choose “No-Cost EMI” at checkout for eligible products and tenors.
2) The merchant provides a discount that compensates for the interest that would normally be charged.
3) You repay the financed principal through EMIs over the chosen tenor.
4) Depending on the plan, certain fees/taxes may apply (processing fee, documentation fee, GST, etc.). These are disclosed upfront.

What No-Cost EMI is good for
• Electronics: phones, laptops, TVs, appliances
• Education: select course fees through partner platforms
• Lifestyle purchases: furniture, fitness equipment (partner-based)
• Seasonal spends: planned purchases without impacting monthly cashflow heavily

Important: what it is NOT
• It is not a “free loan” with no obligations.
• Missing EMIs can still lead to penalty charges and credit impact.
• Not all products or tenors are eligible; eligibility depends on merchant tie-ups and customer profile.

Eligibility (high-level)
No-Cost EMI availability depends on:
• Your credit profile and basic eligibility checks
• Merchant/product eligibility and invoice value
• Available EMI tenors on the partner platform
• Successful identity verification and mandate setup

Charges and disclosures
Even when the interest cost is offset by merchant subvention, other charges may still apply:
• Processing fee: may be charged by the lender/partner
• Documentation fee: in some cases
• Taxes: applicable taxes on fees
• Late payment penalty/bounce charges: if an EMI fails
All charges are shown at checkout and/or in the KFS/schedule.

Returns, cancellations, and refunds
Return/cancellation policies are governed by the merchant and the financing plan:
• If the purchase is cancelled before settlement, the EMI plan may be cancelled.
• If a product is returned after settlement, refund processing and loan closure depend on merchant timelines and lender policy.
• The merchant discount/subvention may be adjusted in certain early-closure scenarios.
Always read the merchant return policy and the EMI plan terms shown at checkout.

Early closure / foreclosure
Some No-Cost EMI plans allow you to close early, but the effective “no-cost” benefit can change if the merchant discount is reversed or partially recovered. This depends on the plan and policy. The safest approach is to treat No-Cost EMI as a commitment for the chosen tenor unless you have confirmed the foreclosure calculation method.

How to use No-Cost EMI responsibly
• Choose a tenor that fits your monthly budget.
• Avoid stacking too many EMI purchases; small EMIs add up.
• Ensure your auto-debit account has sufficient balance.
• Prefer essential purchases and planned upgrades over impulsive buys.
