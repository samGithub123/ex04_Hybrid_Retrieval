About BrightBridge Finance
Simple loans. Clear terms. Fast decisions.

Who we are
BrightBridge Finance is a customer-first lending platform designed to make borrowing clearer, simpler, and more predictable. We focus on practical loan products that people and small businesses use every day—personal credit for urgent needs, housing finance for long-term goals, secured loans for larger-ticket requirements, vehicle finance for mobility, and structured EMI options at checkout.

Our promise
1) Clarity over complexity
Borrowers should not have to guess what they are signing up for. We aim to present your EMI, APR, fees, and key conditions in a clear Key Fact Statement (KFS) and a readable repayment schedule.

2) Speed with responsibility
Fast decisions are important—but so is responsible lending. We use a mix of verification checks and affordability assessment to avoid over-lending and to reduce repayment stress.

3) Respectful servicing
We believe servicing is a long relationship. From onboarding to repayment to closure, our process is designed to be transparent, respectful, and easy to track.

What we offer
• Personal Loans: Unsecured credit for flexible needs
• Home Loans: Property purchase, construction, and select related needs
• Loan Against Property: Secured funding using an existing property
• Two-Wheeler Loans: Financing for scooters and motorcycles with practical EMIs
• No-Cost EMI: Partner checkout financing with transparent plan terms

How we evaluate applications
We evaluate applications using a combination of:
• Identity and address verification
• Credit profile checks (where applicable)
• Income and cashflow assessment
• Existing obligations and EMI affordability
• Property/legal/technical checks for secured loans

What you can expect in your journey
• Upfront disclosure of key terms via KFS
• A clear loan offer with EMI schedule
• Digital documentation where possible, with assisted support when needed
• Repayment reminders and account statements
• Easy closure process with confirmation of closure and required certificates (as applicable)
