Q2. How quickly can I get a decision?
A. Many applications get an in-principle decision within minutes if we have complete information and your identity/credit checks are successful. Some cases require additional verification (income, residence, property documents) and may take longer.

Q3. Do you require collateral?
A. Personal Loans and No-Cost EMI are typically unsecured, meaning they don’t need property collateral. Home Loans and LAP are secured by property. Two-Wheeler Loans are generally secured against the vehicle being financed.

Q4. What is “APR” and how is it different from an interest rate?
A. The interest rate is the cost you pay on the principal as per the chosen rate type (fixed/variable). APR (Annual Percentage Rate) is a broader measure that may include applicable fees and charges spread over the year. APR helps you compare the total cost of credit across lenders.

5) Product-Specific FAQs

Personal Loan
Q18. What can I use a personal loan for?
A. Common uses include medical expenses, education, travel, home improvement, wedding expenses, debt consolidation, and urgent cash needs. We do not fund prohibited or illegal purposes.

Home Loan
Q19. Do you finance new construction, resale, and balance transfer?
A. Many home loan programs cover purchase of ready property, resale properties, construction, and in certain cases balance transfer from another lender, subject to due diligence and property/legal checks.

Loan Against Property (LAP)
Q20. How is LAP different from a home loan?
A. LAP is a secured loan where you pledge an existing residential/commercial property to borrow for broader purposes such as business expansion, education, or large expenses. Home loans are typically for purchasing or constructing a home.

Q21. Do you finance both new and used two-wheelers?
A. Programs often focus on new vehicles; used-vehicle financing may be available in select cases/locations subject to vehicle inspection and policy.

No-Cost EMI
Q22. Is “No-Cost EMI” truly zero interest?
A. No-Cost EMI typically means you pay no additional interest cost compared to paying the product price upfront because the interest is offset through a merchant-funded discount (often called “subvention”). You still pay the principal in installments, and certain fees or taxes may apply depending on the plan.

Q23. Can I close No-Cost EMI early?
A. Some plans allow early closure; however, the merchant discount/subvention may be adjusted and foreclosure charges may apply. The final terms are shared at checkout and in your schedule.
